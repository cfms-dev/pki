The ACME service for CFMS PKI. Connect a compatible client to request certificates for domains you control and automate their renewal.

ACME directory URL

/directory

Use this directory URL as your ACME client’s server address. It returns the protocol’s JSON directory; this homepage introduces the service.

01 / Domain validation

Prove ownership. Issue a certificate.

Use DNS-01 to validate a domain through a DNS TXT record, or HTTP-01 to serve a challenge over HTTP. Wildcard certificates require DNS-01. Public IPv4 and IPv6 certificates use HTTP-01 and have a maximum validity of 160 hours; mixed domain/IP orders are supported.

Your ACME client handles account registration, validation and certificate retrieval through the directory endpoint.

02 / Certificate lifecycle

Keep certificates up to date.

ACME clients can automate renewal and request revocation. The service also publishes ACME Renewal Information (ARI) to help clients schedule renewals.
