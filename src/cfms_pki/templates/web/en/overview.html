01 / Revocation information

Know when a certificate is revoked.

A certificate revocation list (CRL) is a signed record of certificates revoked by their issuing authority before their expiry date.

Certificate validation software can retrieve a CRL using the distribution point recorded in a certificate. Published lists are generated and renewed automatically.
