Terms of service

Responsibilities and limitations when using the CFMS PKI ACME certificate service.

Service and certificate use

The service provides automated certificate requests, domain validation, issuance, renewal information and revocation through ACME. Use the ACME directory with a compatible client.

A certificate records validated domain control at issuance. It does not establish a person's identity, endorse website content or guarantee that a service is safe. Check that the relevant CFMS PKI trust chain is accepted by your intended clients; issuance does not guarantee trust in browsers or operating systems.

Domain control and permitted use

Request certificates only for domains you control or are authorized to manage. Complete validation truthfully and use certificates in accordance with applicable requirements.

Do not attempt to bypass domain validation, impersonate another account, obtain unauthorized certificates, evade rate limits or disrupt the service. Wildcard names require DNS-01 validation; IP-address certificates are not supported.

Keys, renewal and revocation

Protect your ACME account private key and certificate private keys. Your client generates and retains these keys; do not submit private keys as account contact information or request payloads.

You are responsible for certificate installation, monitoring expiry, timely renewal and requesting revocation when a key is compromised or a certificate should no longer be used. Renewal information helps schedule requests but does not renew a certificate for you.

Availability and changes

The service is provided on an as-available basis, without a promised uptime or issuance time. Validation failures, rate limits, maintenance or infrastructure failures may prevent or delay requests. Plan for renewal failures and do not rely on successful issuance at the last moment.

Service settings and these terms may change. When the ACME directory advertises a terms-of-service URL, the client must agree to the applicable terms to create an account or continue ordering. A changed terms URL requires renewed agreement through the existing ACME account flow.

Data and project information

The privacy policy describes the records and external services used to operate this service. Account deactivation changes account status; it does not erase stored records.

Implementation details and project discussions are available in the source repository. Do not post private keys or sensitive account information in public issues.
